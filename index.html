Field guide

Think in connections.

You are the bright orb. Reach the gold star using as few moves as you can. Every move takes one step along one axis.

Inside a slice: X & Y

Walls block movement. The tinted region shows cells connected to the orb without leaving this slice. One slice can contain several separate regions.

Between slices: Z & W

Cyan chevrons are Z doors. Purple diamonds are W doors. Door position matches the direction. X and Y stay the same when you cross.

W wraps around

Up from row 0 reaches the last row. Down from the last row reaches row 0. X, Y and Z have hard boundaries. All campaign levels use three W rows.

Phase, introduced in Level 4

A charge lets you cross one closed wall or missing door. Open moves never spend a charge. You cannot phase beyond X, Y or Z boundaries.

Optimum is the shortest route with this level's starting phase allowance. Normal par is the shortest route without phase. “Optimal” means you matched the optimum; “Par beaten” means you beat the normal benchmark.

Blocked attempts cost no moves and keep an armed phase ready. A successful move disarms it, even if the connection was already open. Undo, restart, and switching levels clear it.

Use Focus for a large map and Atlas to compare slices. Select a slice to inspect its projected landing region; use Back to you to return. Preview Z/W destinations by focusing or hovering a direction button. Every accepted move returns the view to your position.
